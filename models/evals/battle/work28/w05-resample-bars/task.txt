Add resample(bars, minutes) to bars.mjs and export it. It groups 1-minute bars into bars of that many minutes (60 = hourly), each group starting on a multiple of that many minutes since midnight UTC. A group's t is its start time, its open is the first bar's open, close the last bar's close, high the highest high, low the lowest low, and v the sum of the volumes. The bars come in time order; a group with no bars is left out. Add a test for it.
