How it works

In a normal conversation the model may call Math whenever it needs a number. A request that is mostly math (or anything after /math) goes to math mode, which makes the calculation compulsory and checks the answer.

1 · sort
Is it math?

Rules first ("is 409 on a prime spoke", "rods of 144000", "why 19"). Code requests never qualify.

2 · plan
List the sums

The model writes the calculations as Math calls — no mental arithmetic.

3 · compute
Math runs them

The exact answer, its check, the matching thesis claim and its verdict, and a picture link.

4 · answer
The model explains

Plain words, only from the results it was given.

5 · check
Every number checked

Each number must come from a result or your question; otherwise one retry naming the strays.

The side-by-side box is printed by Bonsai, not typed by the model — the verdict and theorem names on screen are exactly what the audit says.

The Math tab

A new tab in the hub, beside Weights, Harness, Structure, Tests, All docs and Help. Every picture is drawn from the Math tool's own numbers (the same code), labelled with its theorem, with the thesis claim and verdict under it. Type a number and it lands on each picture; an answer's "Picture" link opens the right view with the number marked. These four previews were computed for this page — each number was checked against its theorem while the page was built.

The 19×19 board

Read mod 19 the board is a number system: every crossing but the centre is a power of 1 + 3i (shade = which power). Boxes: 1 + 3i and its 90th power, the quarter turn.

units_cyclic · dial_covers (nd) · g_pow_ninety (nd)

Lattices + music

A prime ≡ 1 (mod 4) meets the square grid in exactly 8 points; the flower circle of norm 7 in 12. The tab adds the circle of fifths (7 walks all 12) and the 23.46-cent comma.

card_circlePoints_prime · twelve_points (nd) · comma_eq

How we'll know it works

First: the 27B alone

30 questions — the dial, rods, base 60, the board, lattices, depth, music, and ten thesis claims — scored today without the brain, then after.

  • Bar: ≥ 27 of 30 with the brain, 0 unchecked numbers, every verdict quoted right.

Tests in the suite

  • Proved fixtures: each op must reproduce the numbers its theorems state (96 spokes, the 16 roots, 409 → 49, (3,1,4), 360 powers of 1 + 3i, 8 and 12 lattice points, 7 depth steps, 215 depth-7 numbers from 83 to 1458, π's base-60 digits, 23.46 cents…).
  • The indexes read all 725 theorems and every audit row; a name that isn't in the folder can never be shown.
  • The number check catches a planted wrong number; 40 ordinary requests never reach math mode.
  • The Math tab in WebKit + Chromium at your Safari size, light and dark, nothing loaded from outside; the box in a real terminal.
