3 · The cache

What it does well (unchanged)

  • Inside one request, each step reads only what is new: typically a few hundred tokens, 309 at the median, about 1.5 s.
  • A side job in between (a memory note, the design review) does not lose it: Ollama parks the conversation in memory and brings it back (28,901 of 30,139 tokens reused right after one).
  • Side jobs ask for the same model and size, so Bonsai is never reloaded for them.

Where it broke

  • After the failed tries the conversation was read from scratch: 8,535 tokens (40.9 s) and 14,483 tokens (91.8 s). The tries use prompts of their own.
  • Still slow, after too: near 45k of the 64k, the app dropped an older copy of a file it had read, and Bonsai read all 40,145 tokens again (262.5 s). Not changed here.
  • At your next message the whole last answer was read again: 18,659 of 32,630 tokens, 132.3 s. Two causes at the same spot: the app took its notes off the old request, and Ollama drops an answer's thinking once a new message comes after it.

The thinking drop, tried on Bonsai

A short conversation with thinking in a tool step was 3,407 tokens. With one new message after it, Ollama sent 1,132: the thinking was gone, and the cache matched only the first 871. The app cannot change that; it can read ahead.

What the new code does

  • No tries on Bonsai, so no switch of prompts mid-request.
  • A finished request keeps its notes written in.
  • Right after an answer that thought, it reads the conversation the way your next message will send it, while you read. Your next message stops that read if it is still going and carries on from where it got to. If you reply faster than the read, you wait no longer than before.

In the replay: the read-ahead read 1,466 tokens in 15.7 s during the 90 s pause; request 2's first step then read 534 tokens in 5.2 s.

5 · Notes

How it was measured

  • Before: Ollama's own log of every request since 27 Oct (130 requests: prompt size, tokens reused, tokens read and their time, tokens written and their time), matched to the saved conversation by time; the tries' times from the conversation itself.
  • Request 1's 8-hour pause overnight (the window waited for your answer to a check-in) is left out of its time.
  • After: the same two requests, word for word, on a copy of the folder, with your saved Bonsai settings at 64k as that session ran, a 90 s pause between them as if you were reading, and no memory saves (a throwaway home). Its numbers come from the same Ollama log.

What this does not show

  • Two replays: request 1 took 1.8 and 13.0 min; Bonsai's answers and their lengths vary from run to run. The page's After is the second replay, on the pushed code (main d39bc88).
  • Request 2 in the replay asked a question about the PDF's layout instead of making it; this morning's ended with a page.
  • Whether the answers are as good: the tries never passed, so nothing was lost there, but the replay's answers are not graded.
  • The app's check-in question and your own reading time are not in either time.
