Bonsai’s flow, as it is and as the Fable-style round built it

The same request loop, stage by stage: on the left how Bonsai works on main today (12f8290), on the right after this round. Orange marks what is new. This is the flow as it was BUILT, not the flow in use: the round was measured on 27 Sep and stopped that evening with no gain on the chart bug (0 of 5 on the old code, 0 of 3 finished tries on the new), so nothing was merged and your installed Bonsai still works as in the left column. The numbers are in the round’s report, bonsai-fable-harness-report-2026-09-27.html.

One real request, both ways

“The symbol search dropdown is hidden behind the EMA legend on the price chart, fix it”, at Low, 32k memory. The left column is the real run of 26 Sep (round 3). The right column is what the new code does at the same moments. Measured that evening: the rescue fired and reported, and the bug was not fixed in any finished try.

On your screen

A helper is one folded line with its job, its counts and what it found; its own reads stay folded (ctrl+o shows the report). This is the app’s real output from its own end-to-end test, run against a stand-in model:

> where is the csv header built? just tell me

⏺ Helper  find where the CSV header is built
  ⎿  2 reads · 1s (ctrl+o to expand)
  ⎿  found: The CSV header is built by toCsv in export.mjs.

⏺ The header comes from toCsv in export.mjs.

While it works, the line counts up (“3 reads · 1 search · 40s · esc stops the helper”); esc stops just the helper and Bonsai carries on. /helpers lists every helper of the conversation.
