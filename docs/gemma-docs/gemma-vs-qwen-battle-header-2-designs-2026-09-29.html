Todaywhat the app draws now
  • 28 look-alike boxes: all dashed until they run, with a blue outline on the 27 in line; nothing says which is which without hovering.

Design 1 · One linesame height as today
  • The 28 grouped by kind with a label under each group (Code 1–12, Questions 13–17 …) and a count: “1 of 28 run”.
  • Tests in line are a blue hatch, not 27 outlines; your vote is a small mark under the test; hover names the test and its result.
  • The status is a pill beside the ONE blue button that fits it: Resume when paused, ■ Stop while running, Run the 19 left when idle.
  • + New test and Edit tests stay; Clear and the colour key move into ⋯. My tests appears once you have one.
Design 2 · Scoreboard + trackone row taller
  • A head-to-head score on top (Gemma 0 · your votes · 0 Qwen), with passes and “1 of 28 run” beside it.
  • The line of tests is one control: its state, then ▶ Resume or ■ Stop, then Clear line, together in one box.
  • Every test is a wide numbered segment across the whole width, grouped by kind: easy to read, easy to hit, no hover needed to know which is 17.
  • The colour key sits at the end of the track; + New test stays the blue button; Clear results is its own button.
