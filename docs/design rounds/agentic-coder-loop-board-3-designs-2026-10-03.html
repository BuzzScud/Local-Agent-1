Live preview · 3 Oct 2026 · three loops: debugging, testing, the web · nothing is built into the app yet · keys 1–5 switch tabs
Loops

1 · Board a list, with the picked loop opened below

  • Every loop is one line: its kind, how often it runs, what it is doing now, its last runs as squares, its last result.
  • The lower half opens the loop you picked: its run on the left, and on the right what that kind of loop keeps track of (every test, run by run, or the pages it reads).
  • Good at: many loops at once; the answer to “is anything red or waiting for me?” in one look.
  • Weaker at: you see only one loop’s run at a time.

2 · Timeline each loop is a lane, time runs left to right

  • A run is a bar: green passed, red failed, yellow while it waited for you. ◇ marks when the next runs are due. The green line is now.
  • ← → walk back through a loop’s runs and show what that run did. [ ] change the time scale.
  • Good at: what ran when, what waited on what, and what happened while you were away.
  • Weaker at: it wants a wide window, and a run’s own lines get the smaller half.

3 · Tiles every loop working, side by side

  • Each loop is a small window of its own: its run’s lines as they come in, a bar to its next run, its last runs at the bottom.
  • A loop that needs you turns yellow and shows its question in the tile.
  • Good at: two to four loops you want to watch at the same time.
  • Weaker at: past six loops the tiles get small.

Found while making this it matters for the build

  1. One sentence stopped every edit. My first loop message said “do not change the tests”. The app read the whole message as a question and refused all 17 edits over two runs (7 minutes with nothing changed). It is the sorting flaw this morning’s audit named. A loop repeats its message with nobody watching, so this has to be fixed first, or /loop has to warn when a message will be read as a question.
  2. A half-fix is put back. When a message’s last test run fails, the app undoes that message’s changes. So a debugging run that fixes two bugs of three leaves nothing, and the next run starts from zero. Safe, but a debugging loop may never get there on a hard bug. The build needs a choice: keep it, or keep the progress when fewer tests fail than before.
  3. The plan question. In Manual mode the app asks “Before I change anything… go ahead?” at a run’s first change. With your pick (a run that asks is paused, “needs you”), every debugging run would wait for you once. In Accept edits or Auto it does not.

What the build adds

  • /loop 10m <message> and /loops in the app, and this board in a window of its own.
  • Each run as its own background session with a fresh conversation, so enter joins the real run.
  • Your picks so far: loops stop with their window · a run that asks is paused · they wait while the model is off · they end when the job is done or after 24 hours.
