A /rules command: 2 designs

Both use your real memory, the 16 notes saved on this Mac today. Both show exactly what Gemma reads at the start of every conversation. Today, /memory lists only 8 notes per section and gives no way to switch one off.

What the keys do in design 2

  • space switches a rule off without deleting it. ● on becomes ○ off, and Gemma stops reading it.
  • e edits the rule in place. Enter saves it, esc leaves it as it was.
  • a adds a new rule at the bottom of "Always".
  • d asks "Delete this rule?" first. /memory undo brings it back.
  • s moves a note between "Always" (read every time) and "Other notes" (read only when a request fits).

Same in both designs

  • The count: 15 of 20. A 21st rule asks you to switch one off first, so the list can't grow without limit.
  • An orange ⚠ marks a note that only records something that happened, like "Created notes.html…".
  • The token count is what the rules cost Gemma before it reads your request.
  • Rules you change apply from the next message. Nothing restarts.

Design round, 28 Sep 2026. Both mockups use the real notes in ~/.agentic/memory/facts (15 "always" rules + 1 other note, 1,292 characters of rules). The limit of 20 is a proposal. Nothing is built yet. You can pick one, or mix parts of both.
