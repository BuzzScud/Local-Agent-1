What happens, step by step

    What changes from today

    • No box. Who and where on the left, your last 5 conversations in this folder on the right.
    • The model loads in the left column (spinner, seconds, what it is doing), then turns into a green ● ready with its effort and how long it took. The separate "Starting…" line is gone.
    • The three tips leave the top. One tip sits under the prompt box until your first message, a different one each start (a new folder always gets /init first); then "? for shortcuts" is back.
    • A new folder: the safety check uses the same two columns, and says nothing is read there before you say yes.
    • After your first message the page is exactly the conversation you have now (grey strip, rail).
    What is real here. Every frame is drawn by the app's own drawing code (Ink) at the window size you pick, in Apple Terminal's colours. Today = the app's own screen, unchanged. Your pick = the new top over a copy of the app's screen whose only change is the tip line. Real: your model and effort, what each folder reads, its git state, your conversations in each folder with their times, your Mac's memory when this was made, and your own first messages there (the notification card in ~, notes.html in agentic-coder, the weather widget for the new folder) with their first thinking. Made up: the new folder's name, and the clock: the 31-second first start plays about 3× faster than real.
