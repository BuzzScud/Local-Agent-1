How this works

Every screen here is drawn by the app's own code (terminal/src/app/home-looks.jsx) for each state you can reach: model off or ready, each mode, each item picked, and the start frame by frame. The prompt box under it is a capture of the real app. The arrow keys follow the app's own moves, so what you do here is what the app does. Things that open a panel or a conversation in the app (Switch model, Settings, a conversation) say what they open instead.

In the app: /home goes to the next look, /home cockpit, /home menu or /home cards picks one, and the pick is kept for every new window. The Launcher stays the default until you choose.
