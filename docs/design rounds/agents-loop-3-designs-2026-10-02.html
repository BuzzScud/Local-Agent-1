/agents · the live loop

2 Oct 2026 · three designs of the loop ring on a pretend service · not picked yet

/agents takes one request through six stages: Define → Plan → Build → Verify → Review → Ship. Each stage is a ring of four steps that goes round once per item: a question, a part of the plan, a task, a check, a review area, the reviewers. It never commits: each finished task saves a rewind point. The plan gate is the one yes it needs, and it stops and asks when something on the stop list comes up. All three designs below are drawn from the same live run. Pick the work, press a stop, and answer in any terminal.

as picked

Your pick, as drawn: the ring, with an arrow from the step it is on to what it is doing, then three lines under it and the stages at the bottom.

coding
  • The way out of a step fills as the step goes, so you can see how far along it is. The steps it has finished stay lit for this time round; a miss turns its node red with ✗ and adds one to the loop count.
  • The three lines under the ring: the loop and its last miss (or your last answer), the command running now, and the doubt review of the latest tasks.
  • The shortest of the three (13 rows), so the request stays in view above it.
2

Ring and rail

A rail on the left holds the six stages and every item of the current one (questions, parts, tasks, checks, areas, reviewers), each with its result. A smaller ring on the right shows the step it is on.

coding
  • The whole run at a glance: which tasks are done, which one is on its second try, which doubt reviews held, which task was left open or split in two.
  • Each stage gives a summary when it ends: SPEC.md, the number of tasks, the review's critical · important · suggestion counts, and GO.
  • The tallest (18 rows). When a stop opens, the list scrolls so the current item stays in view.
3

Ring and log

A comet keeps going round the ring while it works. Beside the ring is a "now" box, and under it a log of every step with its time, the way a CI run reads.

coding
  • The comet shows it is working: quick while the model writes, slow while a command runs. It stops in amber at a stop and in grey when paused.
  • The log keeps everything: each step, each miss, each stop with your answer, and each doubt review, so you can read back what happened when you get back.
  • The "now" box has the step and try, what it is doing, the command, the doubt review and the counts.

The same in all three

  • Never commits. SAVE leaves a rewind point after each task; at the end, GO comes with "Rollback: /rewind to point 0" and "nothing committed: read git diff, then commit it yourself".
  • The stop list: the poster's list (auth, migrations, payments, deploys, secrets, deletions, a test that won't pass), the math guards (an expected value or a tolerance changed in a test, a constant from your MATH notes edited), a new package, and a big diff (over 100 lines or 3 files in one task). A stop takes the prompt box's place and waits for you.
  • The plan gate takes only "1 Yes, build it": "looks fine" is not a yes. "Change it" goes back to WRITE and asks again; "Show the plan" lists the tasks in the box.
  • Doubt review after each task: the second-opinion model tries to disprove that task's claim while the next task starts, and the result ("held" or "fixed") comes in when it is ready.
  • A critical finding in Review goes back to Build for one fix task, then on again (the poster's "fix and re-run"); in Ship it is NO-GO until it is fixed.

Pick 1, 2 or 3 in the chat, or a mix, such as design 2's rail with design 3's comet.
