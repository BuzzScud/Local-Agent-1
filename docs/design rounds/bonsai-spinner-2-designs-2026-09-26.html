What is real here

  • Real: the terminal lines. The app's own LiveArea and Spinner (terminal/src/app/screen.jsx) rendered for every 40 ms, with the icon from spinFrame in terminal/src/ui/theme.mjs.
  • Built from measurements: the turn's timing. Bonsai 2 27B on this Mac writes 13.8 tokens/s and reads about 60/s; the phases and their lengths are an example turn, not a recording.
  • Close, not exact: the glyphs. ✿ ❀ ❁ ✻ and the braille dots come from macOS fallback fonts in both Safari and Terminal; each is drawn in one cell here, as Terminal does.
