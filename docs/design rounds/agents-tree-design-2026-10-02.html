How the tree maps to /agents

  • Main session (orange): the model on your service. It plans and decides; the stage pips show where the run is.
  • The loop (green): the stage's four steps as bars (red, green, check, save while it builds), each with what it does. It plays the part of the demo's fork layer: pass → next task, miss → try again.
  • Helpers (blue): explorer (only reads), worker (edits and runs the tests), checker (the suite and the math check). In Ship they are the three reviewers. Each one shows running, done, a miss or waits.
  • Second opinion (purple): the review model from /subagents, on call. It is asked before the plan's gate, after a miss, and before a task counts as done. It reads the whole run, never edits code, and its last advice, calls and tokens read are on the rail.
  • Session log and status line: who did what, and the stage, helpers at work, the second opinion, the guards and the rewind points.

Simpler than version 2

  • Out: the NOW panel, the output and diff panes, the math chart and the guard meters. The guards are one item in the status line; a stop still opens its question box over the log.
